# Forecast output format

Lead with the answer for the selected period and each currency:

1. Won so far and likely revenue, meaning the adjusted forecast including won revenue.
2. The matching target, the remaining amount to win after won revenue, and the
   projected shortfall or surplus against likely revenue. Do not invent a target.
3. Best case means every included open deal closes. Manager commit is an entered human
   judgment, shown separately; flag when in-scope deals cannot support it.
4. The named risk adjustments that explain a gap. If activity evidence is partial,
   state beside likely revenue that the figure may be optimistic.

Show the computed slipped-deal subtotal with its count, open value, adjusted contribution,
and whether that contribution is included in the headline or shown separately.

When probabilities are unusable, lead with open deal value and explicitly say it is
not a likelihood estimate. Keep accepted risk reductions separate from the original
open value. Do not label the unweighted amount as likely revenue in charts or text,
or claim that it predicts meeting a target. Already won revenue can establish that
the target has been met. A missing manager commitment need not add a headline row.

Show the available result before any optional settings questions. Explain a weighted
estimate using the chances recorded in Pipedrive. Missing targets and inactive risk
adjustments stay visible, but they do not block a basic estimate. Offer target comparison
and adjustment review afterward; do not present an onboarding checklist.

Keep probability mode, weighted forecast, haircut identifiers, target source, coverage,
assumptions, and limits in the audit section. Keep currencies and target lenses separate.
Do not sum overlapping targets or infer a trend from fewer than three comparable saved
snapshots. Scheduled results do not offer a dashboard.

The optional dashboard uses the same saved result. It shows a target bridge, one shared
scale for likely revenue, best case, target and Manager commit in each currency, and up to
five nonzero risk adjustments with visible text. “No adjustment data” means that no
adjustment data was available; it is not a claim of no risk. HTML is self-contained,
readable on mobile and print, and has no CRM tool operation. Claim delivery only after
the selected local or native artifact has verified creation proof.
